Qopcha Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Qopcha (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a clothing marketplace that connects customers and shop owners. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account data: name, mobile phone number, email (if provided), and account role (customer or shop owner).
  • Orders & commerce: products viewed, cart items, orders, delivery addresses, and order status.
  • Location: approximate or precise location when you allow it (for delivery and neighborhood matching).
  • Photos & media: profile pictures and product images you upload.
  • Device & usage: push notification tokens, app activity needed to run the service, and crash/diagnostic data from platform providers.
  • Communications: support messages and OTP codes sent for phone verification.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account (including SMS verification).
  • Process orders, payments coordination, and deliveries.
  • Show relevant products, shops, and notifications.
  • Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal obligations when required.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only as needed to operate the app, for example with shop owners fulfilling your orders, delivery partners, and service providers such as:

  • Cloud hosting and API infrastructure
  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, messaging, and storage
  • SMS/verification providers for one-time codes

Retention & security

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for legal, accounting, or dispute purposes. We use industry-standard measures (HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls) but no system is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • You can update profile and address information in the app.
  • You can disable location or notifications in device settings.
  • You may request account deletion or data questions by contacting us below.

Children

Qopcha is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
